💜 Pillar 1 · Emotion × Finance

Your feelings are financial data.

The daily check-in takes a few seconds: how you feel and how focused you are, on one pad you tap or drag. Sam connects those signals with your spending, and shows you patterns you'd never notice on your own.

  • ✓
    Mood and focus in one tap. Add energy and stress whenever you want
  • ✓
    Sleep stages, heart-rate variability and State of Mind from Apple Health, if you allow it
  • ✓
    An optional note, with gentle prompts like “Something small to celebrate?”
  • ✓
    Add a mood to any expense, too
  • ✓
    Your streak and full history, and you can edit any day
  • ✓
    Sam links emotions to spending: “You tend to spend more on anxious Fridays”
  • ✓
    “I don’t know / Skip” on every question. No wrong answers.

✨ Pillar 3 · Impulse Control

Want it? Wait first.

Two tools to break the dopamine-buy cycle. The Wishlist is for things you want: add them, wait, then decide. Impulse Pause is for the urge you feel right now.

  • ✓
    Wait 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, or follow the 3-month rule
  • ✓
    Paste a product link and the photo and price fill in by themselves
  • ✓
    See the price as hours of your work, or as things you know, like coffees
  • ✓
    Sam reminds you when the wait is over, and asks if you still want it
  • ✓
    Bought it? Mark it returnable and decide “keep it or return it?” in time
  • ✓
    Impulse Pause: 10 minutes, 1 hour or 1 day, checked against what you can safely spend
  • ✓
    “Help me decide” uses your own past decisions
  • ✓
    See what you resisted and how much you saved

🏠 Home

What you can spend. Not just what you have.

Home starts with one number: your safe-to-spend. It’s your balance minus upcoming bills and the savings you set aside, so “can I afford this?” has a calm answer.

  • ✓
    Upcoming bills, and what’s left until payday
  • ✓
    When a category jumps, it tells you if it was one purchase or many small ones
  • ✓
    Where you’ll end the month at this pace
  • ✓
    What you saved today with round-ups and resisted impulses
  • ✓
    Quick log: an amount and a mood in seconds
  • ✓
    Hide your balances with one tap
📋 Records & Budgets

Track everything. Without the stress.

Expenses, income, loans and savings goals, all in one place. Logging by hand builds awareness. On low-energy days, import a statement or let Sam log it for you.

  • ✓
    4 record types: expenses, income, loans and goals
  • ✓
    A mood tag on every record
  • ✓
    Categories that learn from where you shop, plus your own
  • ✓
    Monthly budgets per category, and what’s left can roll over
  • ✓
    A heads-up when a category goes over its limit
  • ✓
    Import bank statements: CSV, PDF or Excel
  • ✓
    Search, filter by period, and recategorize or delete many at once
  • ✓
    Loans shown as a chain of payments, goals as a jar that fills up
📊 Analysis

See your money and your mind.

Finiverse goes beyond a bar chart. The Analysis tab shows the relationship between your emotions and your finances, so you can understand the 'why' behind your spending, not just the 'what'.

  • ✓
    Your month in review, as a 30-second story
  • ✓
    Are you on track? Your spending against your usual pace
  • ✓
    Income vs expenses, week by week
  • ✓
    A financial health score: savings, emergency fund and spare money
  • ✓
    Emotion map: how each day felt, and what you spent
  • ✓
    The days and hours you spend most, and how sleep plays in
  • ✓
    A heads-up on days that look risky for your money
  • ✓
    Export a PDF report or your full history
