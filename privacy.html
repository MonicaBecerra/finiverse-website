2. Data we collect

We only collect what we need to run the features you use. Most of it is information you type in yourself.

a) Account information

Your name, email address, country, preferred currency and language. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email those services share with us. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them in plain text.

b) Financial records

Income, expenses, debts, savings goals, wishlist items, payment reminders and card information that you choose to add. Sensitive fields such as card details and personal notes get an extra layer of encryption (see Security).

c) Emotional check-ins

Mood, energy and stress levels, and notes you record. These power your emotion × spending insights. Because this can say something about your wellbeing, we treat it with the same care as health data.

d) Conversations with Sam

Messages you send to Sam and Sam's replies, stored so Sam can keep context between sessions. If you talk to Sam by voice, your phone's speech service (from Apple or Google) turns the audio into text; Finiverse only receives and stores the text, never the recording.

e) Photos of receipts and cards

When you scan a receipt or a card, the text is read on your phone (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android). The photo does not leave your phone, unless no clear total is found and you choose to send that receipt to Sam.

f) Health data (optional)

Sleep, heart rate variability, workouts and, on iPhone, State of Mind, read from Apple Health or Health Connect only if you allow it. Section 4 explains exactly what we read and why.

g) Approximate location (optional, Android)

If you allow it, the Android app uses your approximate (not precise) location to show the local weather on the home screen. The coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo only to fetch the forecast. Finiverse does not store your location or link it to your account.

h) Bank and investment accounts (optional)

If you connect them, account and transaction data from your bank (through Plaid) or your brokerage (through SnapTrade). See section 8.

i) Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive the plan, status, renewal dates and a transaction ID so we can unlock your features. We never receive your card number or payment details.

j) Notifications

A push token from Apple or Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and your reminder settings, so we can send the reminders you turned on.

k) App diagnostics and usage

Through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics we collect app events (for example, which screens are opened), crash reports, device model, operating system, app version and approximate country. We use this to fix bugs and improve the app. It is never used for advertising, and the Android app does not collect the advertising ID.

l) Security signals

Firebase App Check confirms that requests come from the genuine Finiverse app (App Attest on iPhone, Play Integrity on Android). If you turn on Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint lock, your phone checks it; Finiverse never receives biometric data.

m) Payments captured automatically (optional)

If you turn them on, Finiverse can pick up payments for you so you only confirm them and say how you felt. On iPhone, a Shortcuts automation that you create runs when you pay with Apple Pay and passes the amount and the merchant to the app; you can also choose transactions from Apple Wallet, and only the ones you select are shared with Finiverse. On Android, if you give the app notification access, it reads notifications only from banking and wallet apps (such as Monzo, Revolut, Starling, Barclays, HSBC or Google Wallet) to take the amount and the merchant of each payment; notifications from every other app are ignored.

What is picked up stays on your phone in “To confirm” until you save it or dismiss it. Screenshots you choose are read on your phone; only the extracted text (not the image) is sent to our AI providers to find the transactions. If you forward receipts to your personal Finiverse email address, we process each message only to take the merchant, total and date: the message is deleted from our receipts mailbox within minutes, the body of the email is not stored anywhere else, and the result is deleted from our servers as soon as your app downloads it. Payments that repeat (rent, salary, subscriptions) can also appear in “To confirm” when they are due. You can turn each of these off at any time.

n) This website

If you allow it, Google Analytics measures how the site is used. Contact form and waitlist entries (name, email, message) are stored in our Firebase database so we can reply. Blog read counts are anonymous. See the Cookie Policy.

3. How we use your data

  • To run your account and the app's features, and keep them in sync across your devices.
  • To show your emotion × spending insights, reports and recommendations.
  • To let Sam answer with your financial context.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you turned on.
  • To unlock the plan you paid for and handle support requests.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix crashes.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the app is used so we can improve it.
  • Only if you turn it on: to train Sam and our prediction models (see section 7).

Finiverse does not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Insights and Sam's suggestions are just that: suggestions you can ignore.

6. Sam and AI processing

Sam's replies can come from three places:

  • Cloud: Claude, a model by Anthropic, reached through OpenRouter. Your message and the financial context Sam needs (for example balances and categories) are sent to generate the reply.
  • On your phone: on supported devices, Apple Foundation Models (iPhone) or Gemini Nano through Android AICore (for example Pixel 9 and newer). These replies never leave your phone.
  • Offline templates: simple replies built on your phone when there is no connection.

To personalise replies, Sam keeps a memory of facts you share and a profile of your preferences built from your conversations and check-ins. Both are stored in your account and deleted with it.

OpenRouter and Anthropic do not use your data to train their models and do not keep it after processing the request, under our agreements with them. Health data is never part of what is sent. Sam is not a financial adviser: see the Terms of Service.

7. Training Sam with your data (optional)

Sam gets better by learning from real conversations. We only use yours for that if you turn on Help improve Sam in Settings › Privacy Center on iPhone or Android. It is off by default, and Finiverse works exactly the same whether it's on or off.

What we use when it's on

  • Your conversations with Sam (your messages and Sam's replies).
  • Your check-in moods and notes.
  • Your spending patterns (amounts, dates, categories and descriptions), to improve features such as recurring-payment detection.

How we protect it

  • Before anything is used, we remove your name, email, account ID, links and long numbers such as card, account or phone numbers. Each person is replaced by a random code.
  • Health data from Apple Health or Health Connect is never used for training.
  • The training is done by Finiverse to build Finiverse's own version of Sam. Your data is never sold or given to other AI companies to train their models.
  • Training files are deleted once a version of Sam has been trained and checked.

Changing your mind

You can turn it off at any time in the same place. From then on your data is left out of every future training run. A version of Sam that was already trained can't “unlearn” what it saw, but it holds no names or account details, and deleting your account removes all your source data.

Separately, to personalise your own replies, Sam keeps a memory and a preference profile inside your account (see section 6). That is not training and is only ever used for you.

8. Bank and investment connections

Connecting a bank or brokerage is optional, needs your explicit action in the app, and may not be available in every country.

Banks, through Plaid

Plaid Technologies, Inc. connects to your bank on your behalf. We may receive: account holder and institution name, account type, the last 4 digits of the account number, current and available balances, and transactions (description, amount, date, merchant). Access is read-only: Finiverse can never make payments or transfers.

Plaid's handling of your data is covered by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Investments, through SnapTrade

SnapTrade connects to your brokerage so you can see holdings and portfolio value. We receive positions, balances and transaction history, read-only. Finiverse cannot place trades. See the SnapTrade Privacy Policy.

How it's used and how to stop it

This data is used only to fill in your records, show balances and give Sam accurate context. It is never sold or used for advertising. You can disconnect any account from the app's settings at any time; this stops new data immediately. Data already imported stays until you delete it or your account.

9. Legal basis (UK GDPR)

For people in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, we rely on these lawful bases:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): your account, financial records, Sam, subscriptions and the features you choose to use.
  • Explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)): health data, mood and stress check-ins, and using your data to train Sam, because they can reveal information about your health. You give it when you turn these features on and can withdraw it at any time.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): approximate location, bank and brokerage connections, automatic payment capture (Apple Pay automation, Wallet, notification access, forwarded receipts), push notifications, and analytics cookies on this website.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): security, fraud prevention, crash reports and aggregate app analytics. You can object at any time by writing to us.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): records we must keep by law, such as for tax or to answer lawful requests.

12. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest in Google Firebase.
  • Database security rules keep each account's data separate: only you can read your records.
  • Card details and personal notes have an extra layer of field-level encryption on top of database encryption. On Android the key is protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Firebase App Check (App Attest on iPhone, Play Integrity on Android) blocks requests that don't come from the real app.
  • Optional Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint lock for the app.
  • Bank and brokerage credentials are never stored by Finiverse.

No system is 100% secure. If a breach ever puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

14. Deleting your account

You can delete your Finiverse account and all its data whenever you want, on any platform.

In the app (iPhone or Android)

  1. Open Finiverse and go to Settings › Security.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Your account, financial records, check-ins, Sam conversations and bank links are erased from our active systems right away.

Without the app

Email hello@finiverseapp.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your Apple ID settings (iPhone) or in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions (Android). Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies.

Residual copies in our providers' backups and logs expire automatically, normally within 30 days. We may keep minimal records the law requires (for example, invoices) for as long as required.
