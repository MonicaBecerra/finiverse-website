Research · Android beta

Finiverse needs you 💜

Our Android beta testing and financial wellbeing research begin on 17 November. Users on both Android and iOS are welcome to take part.

🔬 Starts 17 November 2026 · up to 90 days

What are we working on?

  • ✨Custom Finiverse-style emojis.
  • 🧠Better mental health support resources.
  • 🎨A more accessible visual experience for people with dyslexia, dyscalculia and other neurodivergent needs.

What taking part involves

  • 📱Use Finiverse as you normally would and tell us what works and what doesn't.
  • 📋Fill in two short questionnaires: one at the start and one at the end (about 5 minutes each).
  • ⏳The study lasts up to 90 days. You can stop at any time.
🎁 Your thank-you: 1 month of free Sam AI Premium.

What's coming next

Soon, Finiverse will include features and support for people experiencing depression linked to financial difficulties, including people struggling with suicidal thoughts. These areas need careful, responsible development, so we're shaping them with the people who will use them.

If you're struggling right now

Finiverse and this study are not a crisis service. In the UK you can call Samaritans on 116 123 (free, 24/7) or text SHOUT to 85258. In Spain, call 024. If you're in immediate danger, call 999 (UK) or 112 (EU).

🔐 Before you join

  • Taking part is voluntary. Saying no or stopping later doesn't change how you can use Finiverse.
  • You need to be 18 or older and use Finiverse on an iPhone or Android phone.
  • We use your questionnaire answers and how you use the app's features to understand what helps. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • Results are only published as group totals. Nobody can be identified from them.
  • You can ask us to delete your study data at any time by writing to hello@finiverseapp.com.
  • Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
