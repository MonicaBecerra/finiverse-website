Why Finiverse exists
“When I was diagnosed with ADHD at 27, my whole life finally had an answer. I also live with anxiety, and money has always been what stresses me most. My brain works differently, not worse. I built Finiverse for the version of me who felt lost. If you feel that way right now, I built it for you too.”
Monica Becerra Founder & CEO · Finiverse LTD · London, UK

Master's in International Business Development (Switzerland). Taught herself Xcode with Google and YouTube to build the app she couldn't find.

How Finiverse was born.

🧭 Chapter 1

A name for my past

Diagnosed with ADHD at 27, and living with anxiety too. Suddenly my whole life had an answer.

✈️ Chapter 2

Abroad, on my own

Living abroad meant getting strict with money. Since 2020 I use the 3-month rule: I wait three months, and if I still want it, I buy it.

📊 Chapter 3

An Excel for my sister

My sister asked me for a spreadsheet to organise her money. My ADHD said: what if I make an app instead?

💻 Chapter 4

Learning as I went

I didn't know how to code. Google, YouTube, and later AI as a second pair of eyes. Along the way I realised I was building it the way my brain works.
