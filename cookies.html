2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) works in a similar way, but its contents stay in your browser and aren't sent to our server. This policy covers both, for finiverseapp.com only.

4. What we never use

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • Social media pixels (Meta, TikTok, X, LinkedIn).
  • Cookies that follow you across other websites, or data brokers.
  • Fingerprinting or any tracking that works around your choice.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics 4: only after you allow it. IP addresses are anonymised, Google signals and ad personalisation are off. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: loads the Inter typeface. Google receives your IP address to deliver the font file but sets no cookies.
  • Google Firebase: stores contact-form and waitlist messages and the anonymous blog read counter. No cookies.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: when you follow a download link, their own cookies and policies apply.

6. The iPhone and Android apps

The Finiverse apps for iPhone and Android don't use browser cookies. How the apps handle data, including app analytics and crash reports, is explained in the Privacy Policy.

7. Other ways to control cookies

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking them doesn't break anything on this site; you'll just see the language reset to English.

  • Google Chrome (also Chrome on Android)
  • Safari on Mac · Safari on iPhone
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • To opt out of Google Analytics on every website, use Google's opt-out add-on.
