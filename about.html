Sam AI — what it actually does

Powerful, but
honest about limits.

✓ What Sam does
  • Reads your transactions, moods, and goals
  • Identifies spending patterns over time
  • Answers questions in plain language
  • Suggests actions (save, pause, review)
  • Runs what-if simulations (Sam AI plan)
✕ What Sam does not do
  • Does not access your camera, contacts, or location
  • Does not use your conversations to train AI models
  • Does not give certified financial or legal advice
  • Is not infallible — always verify important numbers
